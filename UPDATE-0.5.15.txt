Interface and manuals: Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish and European Portuguese.

The six manuals use portrait A4 (47 pages, 33 chapters). Chapter 33 explains local
GPX, TCX and KML export. Export to > File offers these formats; TCX courses use
existing or calculated route times, while GPX/KML navigation exports omit times.
Exports remain local and existing files are not overwritten.

The compact Komoot, Garmin and Strava buttons are grouped under Export to, with
room for future services. They save a GPX and open an import page; you upload it.

The private-roads option remains available and is easier to find via activity
settings. Route settings group private-road permission and access review together.
Allowing private roads includes applicable private-tagged roads; it does not lift
other explicit restrictions. Reviewing access issues can retain existing geometry,
elevations and times. Undo and persistence remain available.

Missing access tags do not automatically block walking/cycling. Motor profiles
remain conservative on cycle paths and footpaths without explicit permission.
Saved access choices, routes, edits and maps remain your local data.

The per-user Setup.exe installer preserves user data when updating or uninstalling.
Save and close the application and stop background work before updating. Back up
Mijn GPX, Kaarten and voorkeuren.json. Data is not moved automatically between folders.
This beta is unsigned; see README.md, PRIVACY.md and KNOWN-ISSUES.md.
